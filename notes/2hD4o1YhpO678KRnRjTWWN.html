The world

2007, the year the record business's model broke in public: Radiohead put In Rainbows on a web page with an empty price box (Oct 10), and the quiet records around it came out on the small labels that would define the next decade — 4AD (Blonde Redhead's 23, Beirut's The Flying Club Cup), Sub Pop (Low's Drums and Guns), Dead Oceans' first year (Phosphorescent's Pride), kranky's 100th release (Stars of the Lid), Temporary Residence (Explosions in the Sky), Hyperdub (Burial's Untrue), Domino (Robert Wyatt's Comicopera), Nonesuch (Jonny Greenwood's first film score, There Will Be Blood). Geographically it is Oxford and London, Brooklyn (Boxer, Zach Condon newly arrived from Santa Fe), Chicago (Sky Blue Sky with Nels Cline), Duluth, Austin (Spoon, EITS, SotL), Sheffield (Hawley's Lady's Bridge), Gothenburg (González) and a Wisconsin hunting cabin (For Emma, self-released July 8). The common sound is the hushed one — piano, brushed drums, fingerpicked nylon strings, drones — the side of 2007 that In Rainbows' second half belongs to.
