Seeds

  • The conceit is in the original description: "following a beam of light through eye → mirror → black → pink → blue → sunshine → fire → forest → retrograde. Built from the DNA of Eva Walker's KEXP Early sets." Every title carries a colour, a light word or a stage of the beam.
  • KEXP on the Sonos, early August 2026: radio sessions on Aug 6 (08:31 Seattle) and Aug 11 (08:35–10:00, 25 tracks unpacked) — the morning-radio habit that made an Eva Walker homage feel natural. (Those two sessions fall in the Morning Show slot rather than Early itself; the show was the model, not the source of the tracks.)

The world

Not a scene or a label but a sequence: the kind of mixtape a dawn-shift DJ builds when the brief is "make the sunrise audible." The beam of light passes through nine stations — eye (Jlin), mirror (Kate Bush, Noname), black (Nina Simone), pink (Charli xcx), blue (New Order, Cocteau Twins, ELO, Solange), sunshine (Cleo Sol, M.I.A.), heartbeats and fire (The Knife, FKA twigs, PJ Harvey, Brittany Howard, SAULT), forest (Portishead, English Teacher, Yo La Tengo, Melin Melyn, Massive Attack, Sudan Archives, The Cure) — and ends in Retrograde, the beam bending back on itself. The palette is KEXP's own: 1960s soul and 1980s British art-pop sitting next to 2020s footwork, hyperpop and Chicago rap without apology.

Selection method

Word-and-image adjacency (title, colour, light) constrained by the KEXP daytime canon — artists the station actually plays, not Spotify-similar picks. Nine clusters, two to four songs each, ordered so the beam never doubles back until the last track. Heard-vs-new as of the build date (bare track ID against both history stores): 4 of 25 heard (Blue Monday, Teardrop, Only Love Can Break Your Heart, Wildfires); 21 new — of which eleven still had zero plays a month later (Heartbeats, meta angel, The Dancer, Red Flags, Wandering Star, Nearly Daffodils, Vitamin D, Yellow Brick Road, A Forest, Retrograde), so it remains a genuinely exploratory list.

Connective tissue & sequencing

  • Opens on Jlin's Eye Am — literally the eye the light enters — then two mirrors: Kate Bush's Watching You Without Me and Noname's black mirror, which hands the colour to Nina Simone's Black Is the Color.
  • Pink → blue: Charli xcx's pink diamond refracts into the long blue run — Blue Monday, Pearly-Dewdrops' Drops, Mr. Blue Sky, Cranes in the Sky — the sky brightening in real time.
  • Sunshine: Cleo Sol's Sunshine and M.I.A.'s Sunshowers, then Saint Etienne's Only Love Can Break Your Heart as the first cloud.
  • Fire: Heartbeats → meta angel → The Dancer → Red Flags → SAULT's Wildfires — pulse, then heat.
  • Forest: Portishead's Wandering Star leads into daffodils, autumn sweaters, vitamin D, a Teardrop, a Yellow Brick Road and finally A Forest — the beam scattered through leaves.
  • Closes on James Blake's Retrograde: the light reversing, the show signing off at seven.
