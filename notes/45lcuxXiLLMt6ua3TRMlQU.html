The world

Contemporary R&B's hushed turn, 2016–2025. The year Blonde and A Seat at the Table came out, an Oshawa church kid named Ashton Simmonds released "Get You" as Daniel Caesar, and a generation of R&B went quiet — gospel chords, falsetto, acoustic guitar and room tone where the previous decade had 808s and gloss. Two cities carry it here: Toronto (Caesar, Charlotte Day Wilson, BADBADNOTGOOD, Mustafa — the Freudian circle) and Los Angeles (Tampa-born DESTIN CONRAD and the Kehlani/Ambré writing room, Alex Isley, GIVĒON, Omar Apollo, the Dijon/Mk.gee guitar-R&B axis), with London (Sampha, Elmiene), Atlanta (Mereba, Baby Rose) and Baltimore (Nourished by Time) in the side chapels. This is a mood comp, not a scene study: what holds it is tempo and texture — slow, voices close to the mic, church harmony under bedroom production. "Vespers" is the evening service.
