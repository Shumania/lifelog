The world

Sonny Moore's arc is one of the strangest in pop music: emo frontman of From First to Last, reborn in 2010 as the brostep godhead whose Scary Monsters and Nice Sprites made the drop an American export and OWSLA a scene unto itself. Then the pivot nobody predicted — Jack Ü and "Where Are Ü Now" turning a Bieber voice-memo into avant-pop, a decade of producing everyone from beyond the EDM bubble, and the 2023 double-album return (Quest for Fire / Don't Get Too Close) built with Fred again.., Four Tet, Flowdan and the UK bass underground he'd always drawn from. By 2025–26 (F*CK U SKRILLEX…, hit me where it hurts x, SOMA) he's less a headliner playing hits than a channel: sets stitched from his heroes (Prodigy, Daft Punk, SOPHIE) and his heirs (ISOxo, Knock2, Hamdi, Dylan Brady), the dubstep he came from (Benga, Skream, Rusko) and the ambient-rave communion of the Fred again../Four Tet trio. This playlist is that whole world in one sitting — almost all of it new to this collection.
