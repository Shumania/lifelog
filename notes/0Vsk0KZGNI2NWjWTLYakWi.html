The world

ECM Records, 1971–79 — the decade Eicher built a label around air and reverb. The Scandinavian core: Towner's Solstice band with Garbarek's cry, Weber's orchestral Colours of Chloë, Rypdal's arctic Odyssey. The American wing recorded into the same silence: 21-year-old Metheny's Bright Size Life, Burton/Corea's Crystal Silence, Abercrombie's Timeless. And the label's world-facing edge — Gismonti's Brazil, Walcott's sitar on Cloud Dance, Maupin's underwater Jewel in the Lotus. The anchors (Brahem, Officium, late Jarrett) are later ECM reaching back in — proof the sound never stopped.
