The world

The parallel Top 40 that never charted, 1970–2011. It starts where power pop starts — McCartney-schooled one-man-band records (Emitt Rhodes, Nilsson) and Big Star's Memphis heartbreak — runs through the skinny-tie class of '79 (Shoes, 20/20, The Records), the early-'80s American jangle revival (Crenshaw, Plimsouls), the literate UK new-pop wing he already loves (Squeeze, XTC, Aztec Camera, Prefab Sprout), and lands in the Scottish/alt-rock afterlife of the form (Trashcan Sinatras, Sugar, Teenage Fanclub) before closing in the present tense with Metronomy. One feel throughout: strong melody, smart arrangement, emotional directness — three perfect minutes each.

Connective tissue & sequencing

Chronological — the tracklist is the argument, four decades of the same three-minute idea:

  • The one-man-band prologue (1970–74): Gotta Get Up → Fresh as a Daisy (Rhodes played every instrument; McCartney's favorite) → Couldn't I Just Tell You (the template Rundgren cut in one night) → September Gurls (the genre's sacred text).
  • Class of '76–'79: Shake Some Action — the anthem that named the canon — into the skinny-tie triptych (Too Late, Yellow Pills, Starry Eyes) and Up the Junction, a three-minute kitchen-sink novel with no chorus at all: pure craft anchor.
  • The American jangle revival (1982–83): Since You're Gone's clockwork heartbreak → Someday, Someway → A Million Miles Away.
  • UK new-pop wing (1983–89): Oblivious → When Love Breaks Down → Earn Enough for Us → Streets of Your Town (sunniest melody ever written about a battered town) → Getting Away with It, Marr + Sumner + Tennant doing deadpan sophisti-pop.
  • The afterlife (1990–95): Obscurity Knocks (the title is the whole playlist's thesis) → If I Can't Change Your Mind (Mould compressing power pop into hardcore velocity) → Sparky's Dream → That's Just What You Are.
  • Opens with Gotta Get Up — an alarm-clock of a song and an anchor he loves. Closes on The Look, the July Shazam that proved the lost-hit tradition is still alive; its carousel organ loops the playlist back to the start.
