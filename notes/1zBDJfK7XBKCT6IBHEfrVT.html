The world

The mid-fifties intimate vocal LP: the moment the torch singer stepped away from the big band and made quiet, close-miked records with three or four players. Julie London with Barney Kessel's guitar (Liberty, 1955), the cool-school women who came out of the Kenton band (June Christy, Chris Connor), the EmArcy dates that put Helen Merrill and Sarah Vaughan in front of Clifford Brown's trumpet, Chet Baker singing at Pacific Jazz, the pianist-singers working supper clubs (Blossom Dearie, Jeri Southern, a young Nina Simone), and the two great 1955–58 Capitol/Columbia statements of 3 AM — In the Wee Small Hours and Lady in Satin.
