The world

The Appalachian close-harmony tradition that Trio distilled: two or three voices stacked tight on old songs about death, rivers, and unlucky love. It runs from the Carter Family's 1928 Bristol-orbit sides through the brother duets (Stanley, Louvin, and their pop apex the Everlys), into the 1960s–70s revival where Hazel Dickens & Alice Gerrard proved women could own bluegrass harmony, and Gram Parsons taught Emmylou Harris the duet as a devotional act. Dolly, Linda, and Emmylou made it a supergroup in 1987; Welch, Krauss, and Dolly's own bluegrass return carried it into the new century.
