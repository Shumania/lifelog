The world

Istanbul, 1970–76: Anadolu pop/psych, the moment Turkish rock stopped imitating the West and electrified its own folk canon instead. Erkin Koray wiring the elektro-bağlama into fuzz pedals, Moğollar naming the genre with Anadolu Pop, Selda Bağcan turning village türküs into protest psych, Barış Manço and Cem Karaca making Anatolian melody a national pop language, arranger-nerds like Mustafa Özkent and Zafer Dilek cutting funk-breaks instrumentals from the same songbook. Then the second life: the Amsterdam/Hamburg/London diaspora revival (Altın Gün, Derya Yıldırım, Kit Sebastian) and Istanbul's own Gaye Su Akyol carrying it forward.
