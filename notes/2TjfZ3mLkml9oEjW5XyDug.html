The world

Italy, 1959–1972: the decade in which musica leggera grew up. It starts in the night clubs of Turin and Milan (Buscaglione's whisky-swing, Bindi's orchestral "concerto", Martino's piano-bar "Estate"), passes through the scuola genovese — the Genoa cantautori Bindi, Paoli, Tenco, Lauzi and De André, who imported the French chanson idea that a pop song could be a poem — and lands on the two stages that made these songs national property: the Sanremo Festival (Donaggio '65, Caselli '66, Celentano '66, Modugno '68, Nada '69, Dalla '71 are all Sanremo entries) and Studio Uno, Mina's Saturday-night RAI variety show, which gives the playlist its name. Underneath everything is the RCA Italiana / Ricordi studio-orchestra sound — strings, tremolo guitar, a lone tenor sax — arranged by men like Ennio Morricone before the westerns made him famous, then roughened by the beat generation of Rome's Piper Club (Patty Pravo, Caterina Caselli, Equipe 84) and refined again by the Mogol–Battisti partnership.

Selection method

An era songcraft comp, not a scene study: coherence comes from craft, period and the studio-orchestra sound rather than one label or city. One song per artist, except Mina (the era's voice — her 1960 and 1966 #1s) and Celentano (the door in, and the door out). Period recordings preferred — Tenco (1962 LP), Endrigo (1962 LP), Paoli (Basta chiudere gli occhi, 1964), Hardy (1963 single), Caselli (Casco d'oro, 1966), Mina (Studio Uno '66), Pravo (1968 LP), Nada (1969 LP), Battisti (Emozioni, 1970); the rest are label singles-compilations of the original masters (no live or re-recorded versions).

Quality was corroborated against the canon, not vibes: Sanremo entries, Italian #1s ("Il cielo in una stanza", "La bambola" — nine weeks), and songs that travelled — "Io che non vivo" became Dusty Springfield's "You Don't Have to Say You Love Me" and Elvis's, "Estate" became a jazz standard (João Gilberto, Petrucciani, Chet Baker), "L'appuntamento" is Roberto Carlos's "Sentado à beira do caminho" in Italian, "La canzone di Marinella" is the song whose Mina cover let De André become a musician full-time, and "Il ragazzo della via Gluck" became Hardy's "La maison où j'ai grandi".

Adjacency is by real people, never Spotify-similar: Ennio Morricone arranged "Sapore di sale" (Gato Barbieri on tenor) and composed "Se telefonando"; Ruggero Cini co-wrote "La bambola" and arranged "4/3/1943"; Franco Migliacci (Modugno's "Volare" lyricist) co-wrote "Ma che freddo fa"; Bruno Lauzi of the Genoa school wrote Vanoni's "L'appuntamento" lyric; Gino Paoli wrote Mina's "Il cielo in una stanza" and Vanoni was his muse; Battisti/Mogol wrote "29 settembre" for Equipe 84 before "Emozioni".

Connective tissue & sequencing

Strictly chronological, 1959 → 1972, so the hour plays as the decade's own arc rather than a mood plateau:

  • Night clubs (1959–60): "Guarda che luna" → "Il cielo in una stanza" → "Il nostro concerto" → "Estate" — crooners and piano bars, the whisper before the poets.
  • Genoa (1962–64): Tenco → Endrigo → Paoli's "Sapore di sale" → Hardy's Italian yé-yé → De André's "Marinella" — the cantautore idea arrives; Morricone's arrangement on "Sapore di sale" is the first hint of the film-score sound.
  • Sanremo & the Piper (1965–68): Donaggio's big ballad → Caselli's beat → Mina's "Se telefonando" (Morricone again, now as composer) → Celentano's "Via Gluck" → "29 settembre" → "La bambola" — the orchestra learns to stomp.
  • Maturity (1968–71): "Meraviglioso" → Nada → "Emozioni" → "L'appuntamento" → "4/3/1943" — Modugno's near-suicide angel, a 15-year-old at Sanremo, Battisti's baroque pop, Vanoni's Brazilian torch song, Dalla's censored lullaby.
  • Opens on "Guarda che luna" — look at that moon, the first line of the night-club era, two years before Buscaglione's fatal car crash. Closes on the Shazam: after twenty songs in Italian, "Prisencolinensinainciusol" — Celentano's 1972 gibberish about not being able to understand one another — is the one that sounds like English, and it's the door Andrew actually walked in through.
