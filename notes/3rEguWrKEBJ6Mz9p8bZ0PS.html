The world

Glam disco-pop and dance-rock — the 2004–2008 moment when Scissor Sisters made camp disco a rock-radio format, and the lineage either side of it: Sparks and Moroder-era disco behind, the UK/Brooklyn nu-rave and electro-pop cohort alongside (Gossip, CSS, New Young Pony Club, Alphabeat, Ladyhawke, Sam Sparro, Mystery Jets, The Rapture, Datarock), and Jake Shears' solo records after. Falsetto, four-on-the-floor, a wink.

Selection method

Adjacency: shared bills, producers and scenes — The Rapture and DFA for the disco-punk root; Gossip, CSS and NYPC for the 2006 Kitsuné/Modular dancefloor; Alphabeat, Mystery Jets, Ladyhawke and Sam Sparro for 2008 UK pop radio; Datarock for the Talking Heads thread the screenshot opened; Moloko for the diva-house side of Fatboy/I Monster; Sparks' No. 1 in Heaven as the record Jake Shears cites. Heard-check on all 15 adds by track ID, by title+artist across Sonos/Spotify/Liked Songs, and by artist exposure. Dropped as already heard or liked: Blind (liked), Ladyflash (liked), The Look, Rip It Up, Private Idaho, Daft Punk Is Playing at My House, Lisztomania, Sing It Back's cousins Smalltown Boy and King, Murder on the Dancefloor, Emerge, Mighty Real; dropped as too likely known offline: Beck's Sexx Laws (65 Beck likes), Hot Chip, of Montreal, Saint Etienne, ELO, Roxy Music, Blur and Pulp. 15 of 25 new — the 10 openers are heard by design.

Connective tissue & sequencing

Opens exactly as Saturday did (Take Your Mama → the roaming run → the Vashon run, ending on Grace Kelly). Track 11, Laura, is the pivot — same debut album as Take Your Mama, so the sound doesn't change, only the familiarity. Then chronological drift through the 2005–2008 cohort (Franz Ferdinand → Gossip → CSS → NYPC → Alphabeat → Love Today → Sam Sparro → Ladyhawke → Mystery Jets), Jake Shears' 2018 solo as the late-era bridge, and a backwards walk to the roots to close: The Rapture (2003), Datarock's Talking Heads pastiche, Moloko (1998), and Sparks' 1979 Tryouts for the Human Race — the record this whole sound descends from. ~98 min.
