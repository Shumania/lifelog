The world

One producer's sound, 2005–2024, heard across the artists who borrowed it: Morricone strings and choirs, drums recorded like a dusty 1968 soundtrack, baritone guitar, minor-key hooks that feel sunlit and doomed at once. Burton came up making beats for MF DOOM, turned a spaghetti-western cue into the biggest song of 2006 with CeeLo, then spent the next two decades lending the same weather to Damon Albarn, the Black Keys, Beck, Norah Jones, James Mercer, Karen O, Adele, Parquet Courts and Michael Kiwanuka. The mid-point is literal: Rome was cut over five years at Forum Studios with Morricone's own players (Marc 4 and Alessandroni's I Cantori Moderni), Jack White and Norah Jones singing over them. Whatever the artist on the sleeve, these are all records from the same room.

Connective tissue & sequencing

  • Opens on The High Road — the most-played thing in the seed and the sound in its purest form — before rewinding to 2005.
  • Origins, 2005–06: Benzi Box (DOOM + CeeLo, the introduction that became Gnarls Barkley) → Crazy, whose beat is a Reverberi brothers cue from the 1968 western Preparati la bara! — the Morricone thesis stated in one hit.
  • The British hire, 2007–08: Herculean (Albarn, Simonon, Tong and Tony Allen), then the Shortwave Set's Van Dyke Parks-arranged No Social and Martina Topley-Bird's Carnies — the years Burton was London's most in-demand producer.
  • Americana turned widescreen, 2008–10: Strange Times (from Attack & Release, begun as an Ike Turner record), Gamma Ray (the Modern Guilt single; Cat Power sings backup elsewhere on the album), Going On, then Star Eyes — David Lynch singing over Sparklehorse on the record that also holds Mercer's "Insane Lullaby" — and Tighten Up, the one track on Brothers Burton produced.
  • Rome and its children, 2011–15: Two Against One (Jack White over Morricone's players) → This Head I Hold (Asa Taccone, who later co-wrote "Feel It Still") → Holding On for Life (Broken Bells' Bee Gees falsetto turn) → River Lea, the Adele track that sounds like nothing else on 25.
  • Late period, 2018–24: the Wide Awake! pair (title track anchor into Tenderness, the piano-pop closer nobody expected from Parquet Courts), the Lux Prima pair (Woman into Turn the Light), Hero, then Cheat Codes — Belize brings DOOM back posthumously to close the loop from track 2, and Aquamarine hands off to Kiwanuka.
  • Closes on Floating Parade — produced by Burton with Inflo (SAULT) — the most recent breadcrumb in the history, so the playlist ends exactly where the listening currently is.
