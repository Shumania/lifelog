The world

Brian Eno's studio in Notting Hill, where a 16-year-old neighbour named Fred Gibson joined Eno's a cappella group and was taken under his wing. At 20 he co-produced Eno • Hyde's Someday World (May 2014) and co-wrote and played keyboards and drums on its twin High Life (June 2014) — Karl Hyde of Underworld being the other principal, Eno's guitarist Leo Abrahams in the room. Gibson went on to produce pop (Ed Sheeran, Stormzy), then became Fred again..: the Actual Life diaries (2021–22) built from voice notes and video calls, the Boiler Room set that made him the face of post-lockdown UK dance music, Secret Life (May 2023) — an ambient record made with Eno, released on Four Tet's Text label — and ten days (2024). This is a family tree drawn by credits: teacher → pupil → the pupil's collaborators, and back to the teacher.

Connective tissue & sequencing

  • Opens on An Ending (Ascent) — the Eno drone; building drones from sustained tones is the technique Fred credits Eno with teaching him, so the whole record starts from the lesson.
  • The room, 2014 (2–4): The Satellites (the Someday World opener, Gibson co-producing) → DBF (the High Life groove, Gibson on keys and drums) → Karl Hyde solo on Edgeland (2013), co-produced by Eno's guitarist Leo Abrahams — the same circle of people, one room over.
