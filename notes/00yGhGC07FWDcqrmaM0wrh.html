The world

Instructor-playlist hip-hop: the Peloton canon is Southern funk-rap (Outkast, Ludacris, Big Boi), Timbaland-era bounce, Roots-style live boom-bap, and the 2015–2024 wave of women MCs (Megan, Lizzo, GloRilla, Flo Milli) plus UK/Australian exports (Skepta, Little Simz, Sampa the Great, Tkay Maidza, Genesis Owusu). The through-line is tempo and swagger, not sub-genre — everything here is built to be shouted over a bike.

Selection method

Adjacency: same artists as the Peloton lane but different songs (Big Boi's solo record instead of Outkast hits, GhettoMusick instead of Hey Ya, Here I Come instead of The Seed), or the same scenes one step out (Skepta/Simz/Sampa for M.I.A.'s London-diaspora energy; Big Freedia for the bounce the Peloton list only had via Beyoncé). Every candidate was checked three ways: bare track ID against all history/likes, title+artist fuzzy sweep against Sonos, Spotify, and Liked Songs (catches alternate releases), and membership in the Peloton playlist itself (a ride play = heard, even though those never reach our archive). Dropped on those grounds: Bonfire, SAOKO, Creator, Disparate Youth, ooh la la, (Rock) Superstar, Tempo, GLOWED UP, Nissan Altima, Ch-Check It Out (Beastie Boys: 97 likes — too risky), Gimme Some More (too famous). 24 of 25 new by ID; 2 deliberate anchors = Here I Come (heard, Roots have 44 likes) and Bucky Done Gun (heard ×4 and on the Peloton list) — 8%, inside the 5–10% ask. No artist repeats from Vols. 3–5 or the 7-/8-2026 burns.

Connective tissue & sequencing

Workout arc, same as Vol. 5: warm-up (Channel Tres' house-rap lope → Big Boi → Q-Tip → The Roots → Timbaland, 1997–2018 groove at 95–105 bpm), climb (Southern Hospitality kicks the tempo, GhettoMusick's 808 rush, De La/Redman, then the women-MC run Leikeli47 → Megan → .Paak → Sampa → Owusu), peak (Bucky Done Gun's horn stabs open it, Shutdown/Venom/Ultimate/Legend Has It are the four hardest minutes, Big Freedia and PACKAPUNCH are pure bounce), finish (Tkay Maidza → Flo Milli → Princess Nokia → Start a Riot, closing on TGIF because it is the one cool-down that still bangs). ~85 min.
