The world

American country-folk written since 2020: songs made after the tours stopped, mostly recorded in home studios and small rooms, sung close to the mic. It has three capitals. Nashville's quiet fringe — Kacey Musgraves stepping off the arena stage, Erin Rae, Katie Pruitt, Courtney Marie Andrews, Sierra Ferrell — writers who work a block from Music Row but never sound like it. The Carolina circle around Durham producer Brad Cook: he produced Waxahatchee's Tigers Blood and Hurray for the Riff Raff's The Past Is Still Alive, plays in Hiss Golden Messenger, and Lou Hazel (Chapel Hill, a Sylvan Esso collaborator) and Angel Olsen (Asheville) live in the same postcode. And the solitaries — Isakov on his Colorado farm, Dean Johnson in Seattle, Joan Shelley in Kentucky, Ken Pomeroy in Oklahoma, Adrianne Lenker in a cabin — writing alone and sending the songs out.
