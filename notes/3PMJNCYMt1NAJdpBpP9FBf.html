The world

Reverb-pop, or what The Clientele's Alasdair MacLean once called music for "the light of a suburban afternoon in October." It is a lineage rather than a scene: it starts in early-80s Britain with Lawrence's Felt on Cherry Red and the Postcard/Rough Trade jangle of Aztec Camera and The Go-Betweens, crosses the Atlantic with Galaxie 500 in a Boston loft in 1988, settles in Hoboken and Brooklyn (Yo La Tengo, Luna, Merge's Ladybug Transistor and East River Pipe), and resurfaces every decade — Captured Tracks in 2010, Real Estate's suburban New Jersey, Papercuts and Cass McCombs on the California coast. What ties it together is not a tempo but a texture: clean guitars soaked in spring reverb, voices barely above a murmur, and lyrics about streets, weather, and time passing.

Selection method

Adjacency by lineage and label, never Spotify-similar. Three real threads: (1) influence chain — MacLean has named Felt, Galaxie 500 and the Postcard bands as The Clientele's source material; Felt's Primitive Painters carries Elizabeth Fraser's voice, Galaxie 500's Dean Wareham continues into Luna; (2) Merge Records, The Clientele's US home, also released The Ladybug Transistor and East River Pipe; (3) the 2010s revival — Captured Tracks (Wild Nothing, Beach Fossils), Real Estate, and Ultimate Painting (Jack Cooper of Mazes + James Hoare of Veronica Falls), the most openly Clientele-indebted band of that decade. Papercuts (Jason Quever) produced Cass McCombs; Kurt Vile shares Real Estate's Matador/Domino orbit. Connective tissue & sequencing

  • British prehistory (1–5): Reflections After Jane opens because it is the Clientele's purest statement — the whole lineage in three minutes. Then the sources: Felt's six-minute swell, the Go-Betweens' Cattle and Cane (Grant McLennan's Queensland childhood, the template for literate jangle), Aztec Camera's Postcard-era letters, and The Sundays' Harriet Wheeler as the voice that carried it into 1990.
  • Boston → Hoboken (6–9): Tugboat's one-chord drift → Wareham's Luna → Yo La Tengo's Autumn Sweater (the organ hush) → Mazzy Star, the West-Coast answer to the same haze.
  • Merge and the Clientele's return (10–12): Ladybug Transistor and East River Pipe are the label-mates; Everyone You Meet brings the seed band back in its 2017 form.
  • The revival (13–18): Ultimate Painting's Ten Street (London, 2014) hands to Broadcast's Trish Keenan — the odd one out on paper, but Tears in the Typing Pool is the same October light in two minutes — then the Swedish Radio Dept. and the Captured Tracks pair, closing on Real Estate's suburban New Jersey.
  • West-coast dusk (19–22): Papercuts and Cass McCombs slow it down, Kurt Vile's nine-minute Wakin on a Pretty Day is the long exhale, and Tomo Nakayama's Let It End closes on its title — Seattle, the newest play in the history, and the quietest voice here.
