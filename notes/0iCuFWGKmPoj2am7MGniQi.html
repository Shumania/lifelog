The world

Montreal lo-fi and bedroom pop, 2009–2019: the Mile End loft scene. Sebastian Cowan's Arbutus Records starts in the Lab Synthèse loft in 2009 with the Silly Kissers — the band that splits into TOPS and Sean Nicholas Savage — and over the next four years releases Grimes (Geidi Primes, Halfaxa, Visions), the Grimes/d'Eon Darkbloom split, Blue Hawaii and Majical Cloudz. Captured Tracks signs the Montreal transplants at the same moment: Mac DeMarco records 2 in June 2012 at "Jizz Jazz Studios" (his Van Horne apartment in Outremont), and his Makeout Videotape partner Alex Calder, Montreal-via-Edmonton, follows. Homeshake is the hinge — Sagar quits Mac's live band in early 2014 and In the Shower comes out on Sinderlyn the same year — and a second generation grows up in the sound he left: Un Blonde's day-and-night home recordings, Helena Deland, Tess Roby, Common Holly, Bodywash, Ada Lea, and Sorry Girls, Arbutus's 2019 signing that closes the label loop. Everything here was recorded at home or close to it; the common texture is a soft-focus mix of slacker jangle, synth-R&B and hushed synth-pop.

Connective tissue & sequencing

  • Opens on the seed: Every Single Thing (Fresh Air, 2017) — Homeshake at full synth-R&B bloom, the sound Andrew spent Friday afternoon in.
  • The band he came from: My Kind of Woman (DeMarco's 2, recorded in Montreal with Sagar in the touring band) → Light Leave Your Eyes (Calder, Mac's Makeout Videotape drummer/producer, Captured Tracks' other Montreal signing).
  • Arbutus, year one to year five: You Could Even Like Me (Silly Kissers, the label's first vinyl, 2009) → Chin Chin (Savage) → Way to be Loved (TOPS) — the same songwriters splitting into two bands → Oblivion (Grimes, the loft's breakout) → Transparency (d'Eon, the other side of the Darkbloom split) → Try To Be (Blue Hawaii — Braids' singer Raphaelle Standell-Preston with Agor, on Arbutus) → Childhood's End (Majical Cloudz, the hushed floor of the scene).
  • The hinge: Making a Fool of You (In the Shower, 2014) — Sagar leaves Mac's band and the second half of the decade starts here.
  • The generation after: I Felt the Evening Come Through the Window (Un Blonde, 2016 Polaris long list) → Deland's Claudion → Roby's Given Signs → Common Holly's If After All → Bodywash's Eye to Eye → Ada Lea's mercury → Waking Up (Sorry Girls, Arbutus 2019 — the label loop closed ten years on).
  • Closes on Still Together, the last song on 2: the loft scene scattered (DeMarco to New York, Grimes to LA, Sagar still in Montreal), and this is the one that sounds like the room before anyone left.
