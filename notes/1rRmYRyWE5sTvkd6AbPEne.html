The world

Paris, 1998–2001, and everything downstream: the moment French producers (Braxe, Bangalter, Cassius, de Crécy) discovered that a two-bar disco loop, filtered and released like a held breath, could carry an entire track. The scene's pop crossovers (Modjo, Spiller, Sophie Ellis-Bextor) took it to UK radio; its root was always Cerrone's 1977 Supernature; and its children — Breakbot's Ed Banger soft-disco, L'Impératrice, Parcels (hand-picked by Daft Punk themselves) — still run the same sunrise.
