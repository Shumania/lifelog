The world

South London, 2017–2025, after the jazz explosion and before anyone had a name for it: a soul scene that grew out of Brownswood Recordings (Gilles Peterson's label — Yazmin Lacey, Kokoroko, Emma-Jean Thackray's Yellow via Movementt), Bradley Zero's Rhythm Section parties in Peckham, and the Touching Bass nights in Deptford. The sound is hushed and smoke-curled — Rhodes chords, half-time drums borrowed from Yussef Dayes, singers who half-rap and rappers who half-sing. Producer Swindle is the hidden connective wire: he made Greentea Peng's Man Made, Kojey Radical's Water, and Joel Culpepper's Sugar Dumplin. It is a late-night, Sunday-hangover music: Andrew found the front door (Greentea, Yazmin) and stopped; this walks through the rest of the house.
