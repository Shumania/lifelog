The world

Rock songs that are talked more than sung. It starts in 1976 with Jonathan Richman counting in "Roadrunner" and Talking Heads finding a job at CBGB, jumps the Atlantic to Manchester and Wire's London art-school terseness, then goes underground in the 1990s — Stockton, California (Pavement), Louisville/Nashville (Silver Jews, Smog), Hoboken — before resurfacing as a New York and Melbourne dialect (Parquet Courts, Courtney Barnett) and finally a whole new UK/Irish wave (Dry Cleaning, Fontaines D.C., Yard Act). Five decades, one stance: a flat, wry voice over wiry guitars, the lyric doing the heavy lifting and the shrug always landing.

Connective tissue & sequencing

  • 1976–1980, the root system: Roadrunner → Once in a Lifetime → Outdoor Miner → The Boy With the Perpetual Nervousness → Totally Wired. Richman's motorik chant, Byrne's preacher-talk, Wire's 1:45 miniature, the Feelies' twitch, Mark E. Smith's bark — five ways to not-quite-sing.
  • 1990s slacker underground: Gold Soundz → Random Rules → Cold Blooded Old Times. Malkmus to Berman to Callahan — three Drag City/Matador poets, each drier than the last.
  • Mid-2000s bridge: Formed a Band → Stuck Between Stations. Argos and Finn, the two great talk-narrators of that decade, bringing the volume back up.
  • 2012–2018 New York / Melbourne / Philly: Stoned and Starving → Pedestrian at Best → Pretty Pimpin → How Did This Happen!? The anchors' home cluster; Vile's drawl is the hinge between Barnett and BODEGA.
  • The new UK/Irish wave + the American heir: Boys in the Better Land → Scratchcard Lanyard → The Overload → She's Leaving You. Fontaines' stomp, Florence Shaw's pure spoken word, Yard Act's sneer, then Lenderman folding it all back into a Southern Pavement drawl.
  • Opens with Roadrunner because the whole style begins with Richman counting "one-two-three-four-five-six" and talking about the radio. Closes with That Summer Feeling — the same man sixteen years later, still talking rather than singing, but now about how the feeling is going to haunt you the rest of your life. The lineage ends where it started, older and kinder.
