The world

Belo Horizonte, 1967–1980: a group of teenagers and one transcendent voice meeting on a street corner in Santa Tereza to trade Beatles records and João Gilberto chords, then making some of the most harmonically adventurous popular music ever recorded. Clube da Esquina wasn't a band but a cooperative — Milton Nascimento, brothers Lô and Márcio Borges, Beto Guedes, Toninho Horta, Ronaldo Bastos, Wagner Tiso, house band Som Imaginário — and its sound (mountain-town melancholy, choral vocals, Beatles studiocraft, borderless harmony) ran from Milton's 1967 debut through the members' solo records at the decade's end, with Elis Regina as its greatest interpreter and Wayne Shorter carrying it into jazz.
