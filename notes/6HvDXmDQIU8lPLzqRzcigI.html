The world

Germany, 1972–78: the axis running from Düsseldorf (NEU!, La Düsseldorf — Klaus Dinger's motorik pulse) up to the farmhouse at Forst (Harmonia, Cluster, Rother's solo records, all with Conny Plank at the desk), the parallel Berlin school (Ashra, Tangerine Dream), and Munich's Popol Vuh scoring Herzog. Eno is the connective figure — he pilgrimaged to Forst in '76, cut Cluster & Eno and After the Heat with Moebius and Roedelius, and carried the language into Another Green World and Before and After Science. Pastoral machine music: steady pulse, no drama, fields going by.
