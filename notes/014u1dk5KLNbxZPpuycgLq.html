The world

Puerto Rico's soneros — the salsa lead singers whose real job begins when the written verses run out and they improvise the soneo over the montuno. The word is Cuban (Benny Moré baptized Ismael "Maelo" Rivera El Sonero Mayor after watching him sing), but the institution is Boricua: Cortijo's plena and bomba in 1950s Santurce, the Fania/Tico/Vaya salsa dura of 1970s New York and San Juan, TH-Rodven's salsa romántica of the mid-'80s, RMM's 1990s Nuyorican studio pop — and, in 2025, a reggaeton superstar recording salsa dura with the students of the Escuela Libre de Música. One vocal archetype across seventy years, in release order.

Connective tissue & sequencing

  • Three call-and-response pairs across generations carry the thesis: Cortijo & Maelo's El Bombón de Elena (Rafael Cepeda's plena, late-'50s Seeco) → CAFé CON RON with Los Pleneros de la Cresta (plena, 2025); El Gran Combo's Un Verano en Nueva York (Andy Montañez singing, 1975) → NUEVAYoL, which samples it; Frankie Ruiz's Tú Con Él (1985) → Rauw Alejandro's 2024 cover on Cosa Nuestra, an album named for Colón & Lavoe's 1969 LP.
  • Fania-era salsa dura, 1971–78: Cheo's Anacaona (often called the first big hit of the salsa explosion) → Ismael Quintana riding Palmieri's Vámonos Pa'l Monte → Miranda's Así Se Compone un Son → Lavoe twice (with Colón on Calle Luna Calle Sol, leading his own orchestra on Periódico de Ayer) → El Conde's Catalina la O — with Las Caras Lindas, Tite Curet's hymn to Black Puerto Rican beauty, as the set's heart at track 11.
  • Rodven romántica → RMM, 1985–93: Frankie Ruiz, Lalo Rodríguez (Ven, Devórame Otra Vez, the Palmer Hernández song), Cano Estremera (A Mí, from Dueño del Soneo), Gilberto Santa Rosa's Conciencia, and the India/Marc Anthony duet Sergio George produced for Combinación Perfecta.
  • Opens on plena — where Maelo, the sonero mayor, began — and closes with BAILE INoLVIDABLE into LA MuDANZA: Bad Bunny's salsa turn, his album's own closing track and the one Andrew liked. 97 min.
