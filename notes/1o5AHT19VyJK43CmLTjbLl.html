Seeds

Andrew's spoken request, Nov 23 10:25 AM PT, by Siri (transcribed as "GarageBand playlist"): a new Garage Burn "inspired by and taking artists such as Scissor Sisters, The Cult and really upbeat remixes of The Cure, as well as modern disco." He was in the Vashon Garage mid-session when he asked: Franz Ferdinand, Moloko, Kaiser Chiefs, Scissor Sisters' "Better Luck", KT Tunstall, White Town and The B-52's had played there since about 10:00 AM (Sonos). Earlier builds in the same lane are Scissor Sisters Adjacent (Nov 1) and the disco tracks already sitting in Vol. 5, 7-2026 and 8-2026 (Free Yourself, Murphy's Law, Holiday, Hypnotized, Dopamine, Physical, Levitating). All of those were left out so no volume repeats another.

The world

Three dance floors that share a taste for guitars and glitter. The mid-80s British alternative club, where goth and post-punk bands were cut into 12-inch dance mixes: The Cure's Mixed Up, The Cult's Love, Sisters of Mercy, Dead or Alive. The 2003–2010 glam-disco revival around Scissor Sisters, Electric Six and Madonna's Confessions on a Dance Floor. And the 2019–2024 disco revival: Jessie Ware, Róisín Murphy, Dua Lipa, Horse Meat Disco, The Blessed Madonna. One producer runs through the last two: Stuart Price made Confessions, Scissor Sisters' Night Work, and tracks on Future Nostalgia and That! Feels Good!.

Selection method

Adjacency by producer and scene, never Spotify-similar. The Stuart Price line: Hung Up (2005) → Any Which Way and Fire With Fire (Night Work, 2010) → Hallucinate (2020, produced with SG Lewis) → Pearls (2023); SG Lewis carries on to One More with Nile Rodgers. Disco producers on rock bands: François Kevorkian's extended mix of Hot Hot Hot!!!, and Giorgio Moroder producing Sigue Sigue Sputnik. Scissor Sisters family: Jake Shears solo. The Cult's 1985–89 run sits with its club neighbours: Sisters of Mercy, Dead or Alive, Generation X. Cure remixes were picked for tempo: The Walk, Never Enough, Hot Hot Hot!!! and Just Like Heaven are the fast ones on Mixed Up (deluxe edition); the slower Lullaby, Pictures of You and Close to Me mixes were left out.

Heard check three ways: bare track ID (24 of 25 new), an any-version title + artist match against the full history including the Spotify archive, and the Peloton list. Counted by song, 20 of 25 are new recordings. The 5 anchors: She Sells Sanctuary (×6, on Peloton), Fire Woman (×3, on Peloton), Dancing with Myself (the song ×17, in Billy Idol's release), You Spin Me Round (×4) and One More (×3, 2021). Just Like Heaven and Hot Hot Hot!!! are songs he knows in their album versions (×9 and ×1); these mixes are new. No track repeats an earlier Garage Burn volume or Scissor Sisters Adjacent, checked by ID and by title. Production credits for Pearls, Hallucinate and Love Missile F1-11 were looked up (Genius/MusicBrainz, Wikipedia); the Cure remixers are as credited on Spotify. Tempo was not measured: the arc is sequenced from knowledge of the records, not BPM data.

Connective tissue & sequencing

Warm-up (1–5): modern disco at an easy pace. Pearls → Any Which Way → Falling Deep In Love → Narcissus, then Kevorkian's seven-minute Hot Hot Hot!!! as the hinge. Climb (6–15): Hung Up starts it; Filthy/Gorgeous and Danger! High Voltage bring the glam guitars in; One More → Does It Make You Feel Good? → Hallucinate hold the floor; The Walk and You Spin Me Round are the 80s club pair; Crying at the Discotheque → Edge of Saturday Night top it out. Peak (16–22): guitars. Love Missile F1-11, She Sells Sanctuary, Never Enough, Dancing with Myself, Love Removal Machine, Lucretia My Reflection, Fire Woman. Finish (23–25): Too Much Music, the Dizzy Mix of Just Like Heaven, and Fire With Fire, which starts quiet and ends on the biggest chorus on the list.
